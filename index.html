I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), advised by Prof. Evangeline F. Y. Young. Previously, I received my M.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) in 2024, and my B.Eng. degree in Software Engineering from South China University of Technology (SCUT) in 2021. My research interests lie in Electronic Design Automation (EDA), logic synthesis, technology mapping, and GPU-accelerated / differentiable optimization algorithms.
