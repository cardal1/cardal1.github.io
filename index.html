Research Experiences

Differentiable Area-Oriented LUT Mapper 2026.01 – 2026.04
  • Designed a differentiable LUT mapper for area optimization by reformulating discrete cut selection as a continuous optimization problem and designing an effective loss function.
  • Achieved reductions of 14% in LUT count and 19% in logic depth compared with ABC's if -a, with a 2× speedup.
GPU-Accelerated ASIC Gate Sizing and Remapping 2025.04 – 2025.10
  • Designed a GPU-accelerated evaluation framework that jointly supports gate sizing and remapping for mapped circuits using deterministic incremental STA.
  • Extended the framework to support buffer insertion.
  • Achieved average reductions of 8% in delay and 4% in area compared with ABC's gate-sizing flow, with a 60× speedup.
GPU-Accelerated ASIC Technology Mapping 2024.09 – 2025.04
  • Designed a GPU-accelerated, timing-driven ASIC mapper that considers load information in successive mapping iterations.
  • Achieved an average delay reduction of 36% with comparable area compared with &nf in ABC, while delivering nearly 100× speedup.
